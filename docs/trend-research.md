# Graphic tee & hoodie trends — Spain 2025–2026

Research for the STATEMENT line (October 2026). Why it exists: the owner said category pages
(Camisetas…) felt monotonous. After the first row it was the same kind of design over and over (a
centred illustration with a small serif saying) and mostly on light-blue tees. This note sums up what
sells and spreads in streetwear now, then turns that into design and product decisions for a print-on-demand
catalogue (Printful EU, DTG and embroidery).

## What the market is doing

**Fit: oversized, boxy, heavyweight.** Every 2026 trend roundup leads with relaxed, oversized and
structured cuts: boxy tees, dropped shoulders and heavier cotton. A big silhouette is also a bigger
canvas for graphics. In Spain the high street sells "camiseta oversize" as its own category (Stradivarius has
an "oversize" landing page, and Pull&Bear and Bershka do the same), and Spanish independent streetwear labels
are built around it.
- Printify, *Streetwear fashion trends 2026* — oversized/structured pieces, boxy tees, bold graphics and back-print tees, "quiet streetwear" with minimal logos, limited drops, a vintage look as the default — https://printify.com/blog/streetwear-fashion-trends/
- Polifino, *Trend forecast 2025–2026* — maximalist bold graphics, statement typography, retro/distressed finishes, vintage washes, oversized fits as the canvas — https://polifino.co.uk/blogs/news/trend-forecast-what-graphic-t-shirt-styles-are-rising-for-2025-2026
- Easson Apparel, *10 T-shirt trends for 2026* — https://eassonapparel.com/t-shirt-trends-in-2026/ *(search result, page not fetched)*
- Printlyme, *Why streetwear brands prefer heavyweight tees (2026)* — https://www.printlyme.com/blogs/news/why-streetwear-brands-prefer-custom-heavyweight-t-shirts *(search result, page not fetched)*
- Stradivarius, *Camiseta oversize* — https://www.stradivarius.com/es/v/marca-de-camiseta-oversize
- Tshirt Lovers, *Tendencia oversize: el estilo que define el streetwear* — https://tshirtlovers.pt/blog-es/tendencia-oversize-el-estilo-que-define-el-streetwear

**Placement: a small chest mark plus a huge back print.** The most-copied pattern in streetwear is a tiny logo
at the chest with a large graphic on the back ("logo a la espalda"). Spanish shops write about it directly,
and the roundups list back-print tees as a lead trend.
- Atlas Stoked, *Camisetas y sudaderas con el logo a la espalda* — https://atlasstoked.com/blog/post/logo-espalda-sudaderas-y-camisetas.html *(search result, page not fetched)*
- Printify (above): "bold graphics and back-print tees".

**Graphics: bold, textured and hand-made.** The rising looks are maximalist prints, paint and brush strokes,
spray and grunge textures, distressed or "vintage fade" prints, and retro '70s–'90s / Y2K references. Faded,
band-tee-style graphics are a 2026 fashion story in their own right (Marie Claire).
- Vexels, *26 T-shirt design trends for POD 2026* — https://www.vexels.com/blog/26-t-shirt-design-trends-for-print-on-demand/ *(search result, page not fetched)*
- Marie Claire, *The '80s graphic tee trend is winning me over again in 2026* — https://www.marieclaire.com/fashion/80s-graphic-t-shirt-2026-trend-revival-larissa-mills/
- ArtSHINE, *T-shirt print trends forecast 2025–2026* — https://artshine.com.au/2025/02/hot-t-shirt-print-design-trends-forecast-for-2025-2026.html *(search result, page not fetched)*
- DTF Dallas, *Spring 2026 T-shirt design trends* — https://dtfdallas.com/blogs/news/spring-2026-t-shirt-design-trends *(search result, page not fetched)*

**Quiet luxury on the other side.** Alongside the loud pieces, "quiet streetwear" sells monochrome garments
with only a small mark: a monogram, an embroidered chest logo, coordinates. Embroidery adds the dimension that
DTG cannot.
- Printify (above): quiet streetwear, minimal branding, embroidery for dimensional pieces.

**Colour: away from the default.** Washed and garment-dyed colours, earth tones (sand, olive, bone, forest)
and strong pops of one colour. Comfort Colors 1717 (garment-dyed, heavyweight) has become the
reference "vintage" blank.
- Comfort Colors 1717 garment-dyed tee (Printful) — https://www.printful.com/custom/collections/welcome-offer/unisex-garment-dyed-heavyweight-shirt-comfort-colors-1717
- Comfort Colors 1717 review — https://www.tshirtelephant.com/resource-guide/comfort-colors-1717-review

**Spanish pride in 2026.** Spain are the reigning world champions (2010 · 2026), and national-team gear is a
fashion item this year (Decathlon, Sprinter and adidas all list Spain 2026 shirts). Pride graphics sell best when
they are cultural and festive (sol, toro, feria, Mediterráneo, "Hecho en España") and not political.
- Decathlon, *Camiseta España Mundial 2026* — https://www.decathlon.es/es/deportes/futbol/espana-mundial
- Imagenzac, famous fans driving the Spain-shirt trend at the 2026 World Cup — https://www.imagenzac.com.mx/deportes/famosas-imponen-tendencia-con-la-camiseta-de-espana-durante-el-mundial-2026 *(search result, page not fetched)*

*Caveat:* most trend articles are written by POD and print-shop blogs. They agree with each other, but they are not
sales data. The Spain-specific points (oversize as a category, back-logo, festival/fiesta merch) come from
Spanish retail pages and are backed by what Inditex brands show. No first-party sales numbers were available.

## Decisions for ROJO Y GUALDA

| Trend | Design decision (STATEMENT line) | Product decision |
|---|---|---|
| Oversized / heavyweight | Prints drawn to fill the whole 12 × 16 in DTG area | New blueprints **`teeoversize`** (heavyweight / boxy / garment-dyed tee, €39.95) and **`hoodieoversize`** (€64.95), resolved at runtime by title with EU production only |
| Small chest + giant back | Series **Gigante a la espalda**: 10 two-sided designs (toro, león, sol, galeón, Alhambra, abanico, corona, +34, varsity, Mediterráneo) and a back "wall of tags" | Two-sided Printful apparel (+€7 back print, already in the builder) |
| Brush / paint / splatter | Series **Brocha y bandera**: 12 designs of rojigualda brush strokes, smears and splatter with the lion / toro / abanico / sol and bold type | DTG; binary alpha, no semi-transparent haze |
| Spray / grafiti | Series **Spray / Grafiti**: original lettering, drips, overspray as dithered dots, stencil lion and toro | DTG |
| Quiet luxury | Series **Mínimo de lujo**: RyG monogram, coordinates, "Desde siempre", lion seal; 5 embroidered marks with ≤ 3 threads | tee / oversize / hoodie / sweat; embtee, embhoodie, cap, dad hat |
| Retro / vintage fade | Series **Retro postal**: '70s tourism-poster style (Costa Blanca, Ibiza, Sevilla, Galicia, España, Canarias, Madrid, Costa del Sol), banded suns, heavy print wear | tee, oversize, sweat, women's, kids, tote, poster |
| Mixed media | Series **Collage**: our author illustrations (art-*, lion-crowned) on torn paper with tape, a brush stroke and big type, composed at print time | tee, oversize, hoodie, sweat, tote, poster |
| Colour variety | Each design names the colours it was drawn for: black, white, natural/sand, navy, olive, forest, red, maroon, ash. The builder offers exactly those when the product has them | `Design.colors` + a small change in `pickVariants` |
| Pride, not politics | Toro, sol, feria, Mediterráneo, "Hecho en España", +34, 2010 · 2026. No coats of arms, parties or slogans; the bull is a frontal head (not the Osborne silhouette) | — |

Print-safety rules for all the art (scripts/statement-lib.mjs): ≥ 3000 px on the long side, binary alpha,
fades as dots of ≥ ~0.4 mm, strokes and drops ≥ ~1.3 mm, screen-print wear as knock-outs (never
semi-transparent). Embroidery: flat Printful thread colours (#A67843, #CC3333, #FFCC00, #FFFFFF), ≤ 4 per design, no hairlines.
